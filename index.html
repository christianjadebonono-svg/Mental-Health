Learn

Mental health matters every day

Here are a few important things to remember.

Everyone has mental health

Mental health is important for everyone, just like physical health. It can change throughout life.

Feelings are valid

It is normal to experience different emotions. You do not need to pretend to be happy all the time.

Support can help

Talking with a trusted person or qualified professional can be a helpful step when things feel difficult.

Self-care corner

Small steps count

Self-care does not have to be expensive or complicated. Try what feels realistic for you.

☀

Take a gentle break

Pause for a few minutes, stretch, breathe slowly, or step somewhere quiet.

♫

Enjoy something you like

Listen to music, draw, read, play a game, or spend time on a hobby.

☕

Care for your basics

Drink water, eat regularly, get rest, and move in ways that feel comfortable.

A reminder for today
You deserve the same kindness you give to others.
You don't have to do it alone

Reaching out is okay

If you are having a difficult time, consider talking to someone you trust, such as a family member, teacher, school counselor, or health professional.

If someone may be in immediate danger

Seek help from a trusted adult right away and contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. If you are supporting a friend, stay with them while getting help from a responsible adult.

This website is for general awareness and education. It is not a substitute for professional mental health advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
